# Poteto judgment delegate

Read the `poteto-mode` skill's `SKILL.md` before starting, including its inline Principles index. Navigate to a leaf `principle-*` skill whenever you apply that principle. Prose you produce follows the **unslop** and **technical-writing** skills.
